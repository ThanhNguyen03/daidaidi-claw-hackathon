# AdtimaBox Case Study Advisor



**Scope:** When sales inputs a client brief or prospect profile, match it to the most relevant case study and recommend a solution direction.



**Output always includes:**

1\. Matched case(s) with alias and rationale for the match

2\. Recommended solution: CShub package + Campaign instant add-ons

3\. Key selling point / proof point from the matched case

4\. Refer to `adtimabox-product-advisor` for detailed pricing and feature specs



**Does NOT do:** Answer pricing questions, explain feature details, or quote packages — that is the product advisor's job.



---



## MATCHING LOGIC



When reading a client brief, match in this order:



1\. **B2B or B2C?**

     - B2B cases: CS-04 (HCP), CS-05 (garage/mechanic), CS-06 (dealer), CS-07 (POC outlet)

     - B2C cases: CS-01, CS-02, CS-03, CS-08, CS-09, CS-10, CS-11



2\. **Channel**

     - MT shopper → CS-02

     - Pharmacy / clinic → CS-08

     - Brand store chain → CS-03, CS-09

     - Convenience store / circle K → CS-10

     - GT / on-premise / POC → CS-07

     - Garage / repair shop → CS-05

     - Dealer network → CS-06

     - HCP / hospital / clinic → CS-04



3\. **Primary objective**

     - Long-term loyalty program → CS-01, CS-05, CS-07, CS-08

     - New product trial / awareness → CS-09, CS-10

     - O2O conversion → CS-09, CS-10

     - Consumer data from purchase (MT/pharmacy) → CS-02, CS-08

     - B2B intermediary engagement → CS-05, CS-06, CS-07

     - Personalized CRM / retention → CS-03, CS-08

     - Digital engagement / viral / earned media → CS-11, CS-10

     - HCP / professional audience → CS-04



4\. **Industry proximity** — same industry = strongest match; adjacent industry still useful for pitch

## CASE STUDY PROFILES



**CS-01 — Global carbonated beverage MNC, mass-market flagship, UTC on-pack loyalty**

- Industry: F&B — carbonated soft drink

- Target: B2C mass, 15–35, active lifestyle

- Channel: MT, GT, F&B chains

- Pain point: Low purchase frequency, no direct CRM with end-consumer

- Solution: Long-term loyalty on Zalo MiniApp — on-pack UTC code → points → rewards + leaderboard + personalized ZBS

- Recommended: **CShub Pro 1** + **Campaign UTC 80M**

- Proof point: Leaderboard mechanic drives continuous long-term engagement



---



**CS-02 — Global food MNC, cooking condiment brand, MT-channel, bill submission promotion**

- Industry: F&B — cooking condiments

- Target: B2C household, female 25–45, MT shopper

- Channel: Modern trade (supermarket chains)

- Pain point: MT blocks direct consumer data; needs creative promotion mechanic

- Solution: Submit bill (AI OCR) → verify → spin wheel → gift

- Recommended: **Campaign Scan Bill 80M + Lucky Draw 60M** (no CShub required)

- Proof point: Captures purchase behavior data directly from MT receipts

**CS-03 — International footwear retail brand, mid-range, store chain, personalized ZBS CRM**

- Industry: Fashion — footwear

- Target: B2C lifestyle, female 20–35, omnichannel

- Channel: Brand store chain + e-commerce

- Pain point: High churn after first purchase, no personalized digital CRM

- Solution: 5 ZBS trigger types — welcome gift, birthday voucher, monthly promo, redeem reminder, private sale by region

- Recommended: **CShub Base 1 or above**

- Proof point: 5 distinct trigger layers — deepest personalization without campaign add-ons
